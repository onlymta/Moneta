# Expendora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Expendora ("the app") is a personal finance tracker. Replace [YOUR NAME] and [YOUR EMAIL] below before publishing.

**Your financial data stays on your phone.** Accounts, transactions, budgets, goals and bills are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, do not collect them and cannot see them. Backups you create are files you save wherever you choose (for example your own Google Drive); we never receive them.

**Ads.** The free version can show a rewarded video ad when you choose "Watch an ad". Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, according to your consent choices (in the EEA/UK you are asked for consent) and Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . Expendora Plus users do not see ads.

**Purchases.** Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that Expendora Plus was purchased; we never see your payment details.

**Permissions.** Notifications (reminders you turn on), internet (ads and Play purchases), and the advertising ID (ads).

**Children.** Expendora is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions).

**Your choices.** You can delete all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. You can reset your ad ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings.
